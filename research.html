Transactions articles

Two articles in the Transactions of the Cumberland and Westmorland Antiquarian and Archaeological Society are central to the study of the site.

Architecture1982

Notes on Brampton Old Church

John Robinson, B.Arch., A.R.I.B.A.

Transactions of the Cumberland and Westmorland Antiquarian and Archaeological Society, Vol. 82 (1982), pp. 73–90.

A detailed architectural and historical study of the surviving church, its fabric, development and earlier form.

Open the article via DOI doi:10.5284/1061834
Archaeology1936

Report of the Cumberland Excavation Committee for 1935: 3. The Roman Fort on the Stanegate, and Other Remains, at Old Church, Brampton

F. G. Simpson and I. A. Richmond

Transactions of the Cumberland and Westmorland Antiquarian and Archaeological Society, Vol. 36 (1936), pp. 172–182.

The published report of the 1935 excavation that identified the Roman fort's defences and principal internal buildings.

Open the article via DOI doi:10.5284/1063075
